About the institute

From a single ophthalmic ward to a regional centre for eye care and training.

  • 1952

    Begins as a small ophthalmic ward within Civil Hospital, Ahmedabad, treating patients from across the city and surrounding districts.

  • 1968

    Designated the Western Regional Institute of Ophthalmology, extending its reach across Gujarat and neighbouring states.

  • 1984

    Becomes a semi-autonomous institute, affiliated to B. J. Medical College and Gujarat University, while remaining part of the Civil Hospital Medicity campus.

  • Today

    Runs dedicated units in cornea, retina, glaucoma and paediatric ophthalmology, trains postgraduate ophthalmologists, and holds outreach eye camps for rural and underserved communities.
