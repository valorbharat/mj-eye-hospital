About the institute

From a single ophthalmic ward to a regional centre for eye care and training.

  • 1952

    Begins as a small ophthalmic ward within Civil Hospital, Ahmedabad, treating patients from across the city and surrounding districts.

  • 1968

    Designated the Western Regional Institute of Ophthalmology, extending its reach across Gujarat and neighbouring states.

  • 1984

    Becomes a semi-autonomous institute, affiliated to B. J. Medical College and Gujarat University, while remaining part of the Civil Hospital Medicity campus.

  • Today

    Runs dedicated units in cornea, retina, glaucoma and paediatric ophthalmology, trains postgraduate ophthalmologists, and holds outreach eye camps for rural and underserved communities.

Departments & specialty units

Care is organised into focused clinical units, each staffed by faculty and postgraduate trainees.

Cornea & Eye Bank

Corneal disease management, transplant surgery, and the institute's eye bank for donated corneal tissue.

Retina Services

Diagnosis and treatment of retinal disorders, including diabetic retinopathy and retinal detachment.

Glaucoma Clinic

Screening, medical management and surgical care for glaucoma, with long-term monitoring.

Paediatric Ophthalmology & Squint

Eye care for children, including squint (strabismus) correction and vision screening.

Oculoplasty & Low Vision Aid

Reconstructive and eyelid surgery, alongside aids and rehabilitation for patients with low vision.

General OPD & Emergency Eye Care

Walk-in outpatient consultations and emergency management of eye injuries and acute conditions.
